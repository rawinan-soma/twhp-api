# PRD: Consecutive Gold award tier and gold-tier enrolment lockout

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Category:** enhancement
**Raised:** 2026-09-17

## Summary

Three coupled changes to the award system:

1. A new top tier, `consec-gold`, above `gold`.
2. A loosened `gold` gate.
3. A gold-tier **enrolment lockout** — a gold winner may not enrol for the next two fiscal years.

The three are one feature, not three, because the lockout is what makes the
`consec-gold` lookback correct: a gold winner is absent from the two intervening
years, so "gold three fiscal years ago" and "continuous gold" describe the same
factory.

## Year convention

The fiscal-year labels in these tickets (FY2566, FY2569, ...) are **Buddhist
Era**, as the programme uses them. The database and the API store **Common
Era**, per `src/schema/fiscal-year.ts` — no Buddhist Era value crosses the API.

| Label | Stored `fiscal_year` | Window (Asia/Bangkok) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FY2566 | 2023 | 1 Oct 2022 – 30 Sep 2023 |
| FY2567 | 2024 | 1 Oct 2023 – 30 Sep 2024 |
| FY2568 | 2025 | 1 Oct 2024 – 30 Sep 2025 |
| FY2569 | 2026 | 1 Oct 2025 – 30 Sep 2026 |
| FY2570 | 2027 | 1 Oct 2026 – 30 Sep 2027 |

Resolve windows only through `utilities().getFiscalYear(year)` and
`getFiscalYearOf(date)` in `src/utils.ts`. "Three fiscal years back" is
`getFiscalYear(year - 3)`. No new helper is needed.

## The certification cycle

The Gold plaque is effectively valid for three fiscal years.

```
FY2569  wins gold ─────────────┐
FY2570  locked out             │ lookback = 3 fiscal years
FY2571  locked out             │
FY2572  may enrol; wins gold ──┴─> consec-gold
```

## Settled rules

### Grade ladder, evaluated strictly top-down

| Grade | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| `consec-gold` | the `gold` gate, **AND** every Answer to a `special == 2` Question scored `"3"`, **AND** the factory held a gold-tier award in fiscal year **FY − 3** |
| `gold` | every category > 80%, **AND** total >= 90%, **AND** every Answer to a `special == 1` or `special == 3` Question scored `"3"` |
| `silver` | every category > 60% AND total >= 80% |
| `certificate` | total >= 60% |
| `joined` | otherwise |

Consequences of the gate change, stated so they are not rediscovered later:

- The `gold` gate is **not a new rule.** `CONTEXT.md` already specifies *"full
  score on every question where `special` is `1` or `3`"*. The deployed code
  instead gates on `special > 0`. The conflict is recorded in BR-23
  (`docs/business-rules.md`), in the conflict table in `docs/domain-model.md`,
  and as an unsettled item in TD-06 (`docs/technical-debt.md`), which marks it
  **Required before handover**. This change settles it in favour of `CONTEXT.md`.
- Against the deployed code, `gold` therefore becomes easier to reach — the five
  `special == 2` Questions (ids 7, 38, 39, 40, 41) stop gating it, and a Cover
  that finishes `silver` today can finish `gold` afterwards. Against `CONTEXT.md`
  nothing changes. Both are true; the first is what operations will observe.
- `consec-gold`'s special gate — `special` in `{1, 2, 3}` — is exactly the gate
  the deployed code applies to `gold` today. So current code behaviour moves up
  one tier and gains a history check, and `gold` falls back to the documented
  gate.
- `special == 3` keeps its unrelated second meaning (one evidence file per
  choice). That is untouched.

### Enrolment lockout

- A factory whose most recent award was `gold` or `consec-gold` may not create
  an enrolment in the following **two** fiscal years.
- Rejection happens before any file upload, alongside the existing
  "already make an enroll in fiscal year" guard.
- `silver`, `certificate` and `joined` carry no lockout — those factories enrol
  every year as they do today.

### Awards are recorded in one table, from now on

A new `Awards` table is the permanent record of what each factory has won - one
row per factory per fiscal year, carrying the Grade, and referencing the
originating Cover where there is one.

Finalize writes a row when it moves a Cover to `finished`. The FY2566 import
(issue 04) writes rows with a null Cover reference, because no assessment for
that year exists in this system. Both new rules read this one table through a
single resolver and never recompute a past Grade.

Fiscal year is stored as the **Common Era** year the fiscal year ends in, the
same convention `src/schema/fiscal-year.ts` uses: FY2566 is stored as `2023`.
See "Year convention" below.

This supersedes the consequence recorded in ADR-0001 — *"If scoring rules change,
old covers are automatically re-scored with the new rules. This is acceptable —
historical scores are not audited separately."* That is now false: two award
decisions audit historical grades.

Live recompute was rejected for a specific reason. The gold gate is being
loosened in the same release, so re-scoring a past Cover under today's code can
turn a past `silver` into a `gold` and **retroactively lock a factory out of an
enrolment it has already made**. A stored grade cannot do that.

## Settled since triage opened

- FY2566, FY2567 and FY2568 arrive as **supplied gold winners**, not as Covers
  and Answers.
- **FY2569** is different: its Covers are in the database and its Score Reports
  are live endpoints, so **every** grade is supplied, not only golds. The
  auditors' record is the source of truth - nothing is recomputed, and neither
  gold gate is involved.
- Unmatched factories are **skipped**, not created.
- The three-year gold validity is **existing policy**, already enforced by the
  programme manager, so the supplied history respects it and the two new rules
  cannot contradict each other.
- A FY - 3 `consec-gold` counts as gold for the lookback.
