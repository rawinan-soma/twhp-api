# 03 — Block enrolment for two fiscal years after a gold-tier award

**Status:** closed
**Category:** enhancement
**Depends on:** 01

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** A factory that wins `gold` or `consec-gold` may not create a new enrolment for the following two fiscal years.

**Current behavior:**
A factory may enrol once per fiscal year. Enrolment creation rejects with 400
and `"already make an enroll in fiscal year"` when an enrolment already exists
in the current fiscal-year window. Nothing else restricts enrolment, and no past
award is consulted. A factory that won gold last year can enrol again this year.

**Desired behavior:**
The Gold plaque is valid for three fiscal years. A factory whose most recent
award was `gold` or `consec-gold` is blocked from enrolling in the next two
fiscal years, and may enrol again in the third.

```
FY2569  wins gold
FY2570  blocked
FY2571  blocked
FY2572  may enrol
```

Concretely, creating an enrolment in fiscal year Y is rejected when the `Awards`
table (issue 01) holds a row for that factory, graded `gold` or `consec-gold`,
in fiscal year Y − 1 or Y − 2.

- `silver`, `certificate` and `joined` carry no lockout.
- No `Awards` row — no award recorded — carries no lockout.
- The check runs **before any file is uploaded**, alongside the existing
  duplicate-enrolment guard. File I/O happens outside transactions and after
  validation in this codebase; a rejected enrolment must not leave orphaned
  objects in storage.
- Rejection is 400, consistent with the existing guard on this route.

**Key interfaces:**
- The enrolment creation service gains a second rejection path next to the
  duplicate-enrolment guard, returning the framework's status response rather
  than throwing.
- The enrolment creation route already declares a union of 400 message shapes;
  the new message is added as a further variant so the OpenAPI contract stays
  accurate.
- The enrolment's fiscal year Y comes from `utilities().getFiscalYear()`. Y − 1
  and Y − 2 are plain integer arithmetic on the Common Era `fiscal_year`.
- The gold-tier lookup goes through the **same resolver issue 02 uses** - "did
  this factory hold a gold-tier award in fiscal year Y". One owner, as
  `coverStatus.ts` owns latest-Cover-status resolution under ADR-0010. A second
  ad-hoc query over `Awards` is a review failure.
- The rejection message is a public contract surfaced to the factory by the
  frontend. It should say both *why* the factory is blocked and *when* it may
  enrol again. **The exact Thai wording must be supplied by the maintainer.**
  Until then use a clear English placeholder naming the next eligible fiscal
  year, and flag it in the PR description.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A factory with a stored `gold` in FY − 1 is rejected with 400 when it
      tries to enrol.
- [ ] A factory with a stored `gold` in FY − 2 is rejected with 400.
- [ ] A factory whose stored `gold` is in FY − 3 enrols successfully.
- [ ] A stored `consec-gold` blocks identically to `gold` in both years.
- [ ] A factory with `silver`, `certificate` or `joined` in FY − 1 enrols
      successfully.
- [ ] A factory with an enrolment but no `finished` Cover in FY − 1 enrols
      successfully.
- [ ] A factory enrolling for the first time is unaffected.
- [ ] The existing duplicate-enrolment rejection still fires and its message is
      unchanged.
- [ ] No file is uploaded to object storage when an enrolment is rejected by the
      lockout.
- [ ] The rejection names the next fiscal year in which the factory may enrol.

**Out of scope:**
- Grade computation — issues 01 and 02.
- Any change to enrolment update or deletion; the lockout guards creation only.
- Any administrative override allowing a locked-out factory to enrol anyway. If
  one is needed it is a separate request — see the open question below.
- Notifying a factory when its lockout expires.

## Note on imported history

The three-year gold validity is **existing policy**, enforced by the programme
manager before this change encodes it. The imported award history (issue 04)
therefore already respects it: no factory should hold gold in two fiscal years
less than three apart.

That makes the import safe, and it is worth asserting rather than assuming. After
the import, check that no factory holds two gold-tier awards within three fiscal
years of each other. A violation means the policy was not enforced for that
factory, and the lockout would then block an enrolment the programme manager
expects to accept.

Enrolment for FY2570 onward is also gatekept by the programme manager directly.
This guard backs that process up; it is not the only thing standing between a
gold winner and a second enrolment.

## Open question, non-blocking

Is there any override — an admin or evaluator re-opening enrolment for a
locked-out factory? Built as specified there is none: a gold winner cannot enrol
for two years under any circumstance. Worth confirming before release, because
it is easier to add an override than to retract one.
