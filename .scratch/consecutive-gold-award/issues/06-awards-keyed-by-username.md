## Decision (maintainer, 2026-11-04)

Some factories that won in FY2566–68 have **no account yet**, so `Awards.factory_id` (FK to
`Factories.account_id`) cannot hold them. Replace it with **plain `username text`, no FK**, plus a
verify query that lists awards whose username matches no account.

Consequence: "only include factories that exist in the system" (ticket 04 Part A) no longer holds.
The DB stops enforcing existence; the verify query is the only guard. A username spelled
differently from the one the factory later registers with silently loses its history.
