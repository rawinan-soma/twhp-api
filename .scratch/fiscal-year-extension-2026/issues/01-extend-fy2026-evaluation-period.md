# 01 — Extend the FY2026 evaluation period to 15 Oct and withhold grades until it ends

Status: closed
Category: enhancement
Delivery: **temporary hotfix**. Branch off `main`, PR into `main`. The code is **one commit**. Do **not** merge it
into `dev`. Must be deployed before 2026-10-01 00:00 Bangkok. It is reverted on 2026-10-16 (see Revert plan).

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Request

The auditors cannot finish FY2026 evaluation by 30 Sep. They need the evaluation period extended to
15 Oct 2026. Until the period ends, factories must not be told their grade.

## Decisions (triage, 2026-09-24)

1. **The enrollment year still rolls over on 1 Oct.** Enrollment in 1–15 Oct counts toward FY2027 as usual. Enrollment is not
   blocked in code; the program manager tells factories that enrollment is not open yet.
2. **Only reviewer-side reads stay on FY2026 until 15 Oct.** Factory-side reads and writes switch to FY2027 on
   1 Oct as they do today.
   - 2b. **Hard rejects are blocked during 1–15 Oct** (revised 2026-09-24; it was "allowed" at first). A Cover
     hard-rejected in that window would go back to the factory, which can no longer see it. The factory would get an
     in-progress email it cannot act on, and the Cover could never finish. During 1–15 Oct reviewers can only
     approve or change the score.
3. **The results email is off from deployment until the period ends,** not only during 1–15 Oct.
4. **Withheld emails are deferred, not dropped,** but this hotfix only suppresses them. Sending them later
   is a separate issue on `dev`, which depends on grades being persisted there. The hotfix records nothing
   about who was skipped; the later job finds them from FY2026 Covers finalized as `finished`.
5. **The factory's own score read hides the grade** until the period ends.
   - 5b. Only the grade. The `scoring` breakdown stays visible.
6. **Driven by one optional env var**, not a hard-coded date. It doubles as a kill switch: unset it and redeploy
   to end the period early.
7. **The fix is temporary.** On 2026-10-16 it is reverted on `main`, and `dev`'s new features are merged into `main`.
   Nothing from this fix should survive into `dev`.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Add an optional `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END` setting that keeps reviewer lists on the old fiscal year until it passes, and withholds the grade from factories (email and factory score read) until then.

**Current behavior:**
- `utilities().getFiscalYear()` returns a half-open `[Oct 1, next Oct 1)` interval computed from the host-local
  "now". Every caller is fiscal-scoped by it, both factory-side and reviewer-side. On 1 Oct, every
  staff list switches to the new, empty fiscal year.
- Finalizing a Cover to `finished` enqueues a `verdict-result-finished` email job that carries the grade.
- `scoreService.getScoreByFactory` returns `grade` whenever the Cover is `finished`.

**Desired behavior:**

*Config*
- New optional env var `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END`, validated in the central config module like every other
  var. Format `YYYY-MM-DD`. It means host-local (Bangkok) midnight at the **start** of that day, as an
  exclusive bound. `2026-10-16` therefore means "evaluation runs through 15 Oct".
- Unset or empty → today's behavior, byte-for-byte. Malformed → startup throws, like other config errors.
- "The period is open" means: the var is set **and** now < `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END`.

*Reviewer fiscal scope*
- During the extension window, reviewer-side fiscal scoping resolves to the fiscal year that ended at the rollover
  (the fiscal year containing `rollover − 1 ms`, where `rollover` = the start of the fiscal year containing
  `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END`). The extension window is `[rollover, EVALUATION_PERIOD_END)`; an end exactly on
  Oct 1 makes it empty. Otherwise it resolves at now. With the var set to `2026-10-16`, reviewers see
  FY2026 (`[2025-10-01, 2026-10-01)`) until 15 Oct 23:59:59 and FY2027 from 16 Oct. Before 1 Oct nothing changes.
  (Corrected during implementation: an earlier draft said `min(now, END − 1 ms)`, which resolves to FY2027 on 5 Oct.)
- Reviewer-side = the staff list queries under `admins/`, `evaluators/`, `provincialOfficers/`:
  - the enroll lists (`listEnrolls`)
  - the score lists (`listScoreReports`)
  - the factory lists' `enrolled=true` fiscal filter (`enrollExists` with `withFiscalYear`)
- Factory-side callers are unchanged, and still roll over on 1 Oct: enroll create/update/read, cover
  create/read, answer save/submit/read/update/negotiate, and `getScoreByFactory`'s cover lookup.
- Covers are reviewed and finalized by id through `evaluatorReviewService` (`getAnswers`, `saveAnswerVerdict`,
  `finalize`). These calls are not fiscal-scoped and need no change. Confirm that before relying on it.
- Keep the fiscal-year rule owned in one place. Extend `getFiscalYear` (for example, an optional reference-date
  parameter) or add a sibling helper beside it. Don't hand-roll date boundaries at the call sites.

*Results email*
- While the period is open, finalizing to `finished` does **not** enqueue `verdict-result-finished`. Log
  one line with the coverId instead, so a skipped send is visible in logs. The finalize response, including
  `grade` returned to the reviewer, is unchanged.
- Suppress at the enqueue site in the API, **not** in the worker. That way the hotfix ships without rebuilding the
  production worker binary.
- `verdict-result-in-progress` is unaffected. It carries no score.

*Hard reject block*
- "The extension window" means: the period is open **and** now is on or after the reviewer fiscal year's end.
  With the var = `2026-10-16`, that is 2026-10-01 00:00 up to 2026-10-16 00:00. Before 1 Oct, hard rejects work as today.
- During the extension window, `saveAnswerVerdict` with `decision: "reject"` returns **400** and writes no log.
  The message tells the reviewer to approve or change the score instead. `approve` and `change_score` are unaffected.
- During the extension window, `finalize` returns **400** when any Answer's latest log is a hard reject. This covers
  rejects saved before 1 Oct but not yet finalized. It must fail before any MinIO deletion, DB write or email. The
  response names the offending answer ids, so the reviewer can re-save them as approve or change_score. The
  existing edit guard already lets a category-scoped reviewer overwrite a `rejected` Answer.
- The hard-reject classification is ADR-0012's (`status = 'rejected'` AND `verdict_choice IS NULL`). Reuse it; don't
  narrow it to a status test.

*Factory score read*
- While the period is open, `getScoreByFactory` returns `grade: null` even for a `finished` Cover. `scoring`
  and all other fields are unchanged. Response schema is unchanged (`grade` is already nullable).
- Staff score lists keep returning `grade`.

**Key interfaces:**
- Config: new optional `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END` (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Add it to the env example or `docker.env`
  documentation, and pass it through every compose service that runs the API.
- `utilities().getFiscalYear()`: needs a way to resolve the interval at a given instant. The reviewer-side
  resolution should be one named helper, not repeated arithmetic.
- A single predicate, for example `isEvaluationPeriodOpen(now)`, is shared by the email suppression and the
  grade redaction, so the two cannot disagree. A second predicate, for example `isExtensionWindow(now)`, gates both
  hard-reject guards and is built from the same helpers.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] With `EVALUATION_PERIOD_END` unset, every existing isolated test passes unchanged, and staff lists, email
      enqueue and factory score behave exactly as before.
- [ ] Config: a valid date parses. A malformed value throws at startup. An empty value is treated as unset. Covered in
      the config tests.
- [ ] With the var = `2026-10-16`, the reviewer fiscal interval resolves to `[2025-10-01, 2026-10-01)` at
      2026-09-24, 2026-10-01 00:00, and 2026-10-15 23:59. It resolves to `[2026-10-01, 2027-10-01)` at 2026-10-16 00:00.
      Unit-tested with injected times.
- [ ] Factory-side fiscal interval at 2026-10-01 00:00 is `[2026-10-01, 2027-10-01)` regardless of the var.
- [ ] While the period is open, finalizing a Cover to `finished` enqueues no email job and logs the skip. After it ends,
      the email is enqueued as today. The in-progress email is enqueued in both cases.
- [ ] While the period is open, `GET factories/assessments/score` for a `finished` Cover returns `grade: null`
      and the unchanged `scoring`. After the period ends, it returns the grade.
- [ ] Staff score lists still include `grade` while the period is open.
- [ ] During the extension window, saving a `reject` verdict returns 400 and adds no answer log. `approve` and
      `change_score` still save. Before 1 Oct, `reject` saves as today.
- [ ] During the extension window, finalizing a Cover that holds a hard-rejected Answer returns 400. The Cover status,
      the answers, the evidence files and the email queue are all untouched. Once those Answers are re-saved as
      approve or change_score, finalize succeeds.
- [ ] Every code site this change touches carries a one-line `TEMPORARY (FY2026 extension, revert 2026-10-16)`
      comment. **Exception** (amended 2026-09-24 after clearance, accepted in code review `5d025a7`): import lines,
      call sites that only forward the period to a helper that carries the comment (e.g. `enrollExists(..., period)`),
      and test-helper plumbing need no comment of their own; the single-commit revert removes them regardless.
      No ADR, `CONTEXT.md`, business-rules, API-conventions or `CLAUDE.md` edits, since the change is reverted
      in two weeks and its record lives in this issue.
- [ ] The whole code change is a single commit, so `git revert <sha>` removes it cleanly. This issue file is
      committed separately so the revert keeps the record.
- [ ] Isolated suite and `biome check src` are clean. Integration tests are run only against a disposable DB.

**Out of scope:**
- Sending the withheld results emails later. That is a separate issue on `dev`, after grade persistence.
- Blocking or gating FY2027 enrollment during 1–15 Oct.
- Giving factories access to FY2026 Covers after 1 Oct.
- Covers hard-rejected and finalized **before** 1 Oct that the factory hasn't resubmitted by 1 Oct. They are also
  unreachable for the factory after rollover. The program manager handles them.
- Any worker change or worker image rebuild.
- Durable docs (ADR, `CONTEXT.md`, guides) — this is a temporary change.
- Merging this fix into `dev`.
- The known timezone risk at the fiscal boundary (`docs/database.md`, "Dates, fiscal years, and timezone").
  Match `getFiscalYear`'s existing host-local behavior; don't fix it here.
