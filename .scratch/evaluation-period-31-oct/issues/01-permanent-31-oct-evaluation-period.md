# 01 — Permanent 31 Oct evaluation period for staff review

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Category:** enhancement

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** For every fiscal year, reviewers may keep auditing the year that just ended until 1 Nov 00:00 Bangkok (through 31 Oct of the next fiscal year). Factories get no such window.

**Desired behavior:**
- The window is `EVALUATION_DAYS = 31` after the 1 Oct rollover, relative to the rollover so it recurs every year. Never literal dates.
- The nine staff list endpoints (`/{admins,evaluators,provincialOfficers}/{factories,enrolls,score}`) default to the year under evaluation when `?fiscalYear` is omitted, during 1–31 Oct. An explicit `?fiscalYear` always wins. Outside the window they default to the current year.
- Every reviewer level (Mental, DOH, ODPC) may save an Answer verdict on the year under evaluation during the window. Outside it a closed year stays ODPC-only (403 `fiscal year N is closed; only ODPC may write`).
- `finalize` stays ODPC-only (ADR-0003).
- A hard reject (`status = 'rejected'` and `verdict_choice IS NULL`) is refused with 400 during the window, on verdict save and on finalize. Approve and change_score are allowed. Finalize refuses before any file deletion, DB write or email.
- Factories have no grace: they may only write the current fiscal year; any other year returns 403 `fiscal year N is closed to factories`, including the year that just ended. The old 31-day factory grace (`GRACE_DAYS`, `factoryGraceApplies`) is removed.
- Factory-side reads keep `getFiscalYear()`.
- Results emails and the factory score read (grade) are unchanged.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `evaluationPeriodApplies(year, now)` is true only for the immediately preceding fiscal year and only before rollover + 31 days (1 Nov 00:00 Bangkok); false at that instant and after, and false for any other year.
2. `getReviewerFiscalYear()` returns the prior year during 1–31 Oct and the current year otherwise; an explicit year always wins.
3. The staff lists for enrolls, score and factories default to the prior year inside the window and the current year outside it.
4. Mental and DOH can save a verdict on the prior year inside the window and get 403 outside it; ODPC is admitted always.
5. A hard reject on verdict save returns 400 `hard reject is closed during the evaluation period` inside the window and is allowed from 1 Nov.
6. `finalize` with a hard-reject Answer inside the window returns 400 naming the Answer ids, before any file is deleted or DB row written; non-ODPC finalize is still refused for level.
7. A factory write to the year that just ended is refused with 403 at any time, including 1–31 Oct; the current year is writable.
8. `GRACE_DAYS` and `factoryGraceApplies` no longer exist.
9. Docs match: `docs/api-conventions.md`, `docs/business-rules.md` (BR-06, BR-06a, BR-06b), ADR-0012 amendment, `CLAUDE.md`.
10. The isolated test files pass (see `CLAUDE.md` Commands) and `bun ./node_modules/.bin/biome check src` reports no errors.
